INSTALLING CLIPKEEP

1. Drag ClipKeep onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open ClipKeep from your Applications folder.


IF MACOS SAYS IT "COULD NOT VERIFY" CLIPKEEP

ClipKeep is free and isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so macOS
warns about it the first time. You only have to allow it once:

1. In the warning, click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to the Security section. Next to the message about ClipKeep,
   click "Open Anyway", then enter your password.
4. Open ClipKeep again and click "Open Anyway" in the last prompt.

ClipKeep opens normally from then on.


OR SKIP THE WARNING: INSTALL FROM TERMINAL

Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities) and paste this line:

  curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Hamza-Boulila/ClipKeep/main/install.sh | bash

It downloads the latest ClipKeep, checks it, puts it in Applications and
opens it.


AFTER IT OPENS

ClipKeep lives in the menu bar (top right of the screen); it has no Dock icon.
Click its icon or press Shift-Command-V to see your clipboard history.
To let it paste for you, allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Accessibility.
